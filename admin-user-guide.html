1. Getting Into the Admin Panel

Before you can do anything, you need to log in. Here's how to get to your admin area.

How to Log In

  1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari).
  2. Go to the OG Farms website address and add /login at the end (your manager will give you the full address).
  3. Type in your email address and password.
  4. Click the Login button.
  5. Once logged in, go to the website address and add /admin at the end — this takes you to the admin area.
💡
Tip: Bookmark the admin page so you can get back to it quickly next time. Just go to /admin and save it in your browser favourites.
⚠️
Important: If you see a message saying "Access Denied" or you get sent back to the home page, it means your account hasn't been set up as an admin yet. Ask your manager to give you admin access in Settings.

Finding Your Way Around

  1. Once you're in the admin area, you'll see a menu on the left side of the screen.
  2. Click any menu item to go to that section — for example, click Products to manage products.
  3. To go back to the main overview at any time, click Dashboard at the top of the menu.

2. The Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home screen. Every time you open the admin area, this is the first thing you'll see. It gives you a quick snapshot of how the business is doing.

What You'll See on the Dashboard

  1. At the top you'll see 4 summary boxes — these update automatically so you always see live numbers.
  2. Total Stores — how many store locations you have active.
  3. Pending Orders — orders that customers have placed and are waiting to be handled.
  4. Low Stock Items — products that are running out and need to be restocked soon.
  5. Total Revenue — the total amount of money earned from all sales (shown in Rands).

Quick Action Buttons

  1. Below the summary boxes you'll see 3 quick buttons.
  2. Click "Add New Store" to quickly jump to adding a new store location.
  3. Click "Manage Stock" to jump to the inventory/stock page.
  4. Click "View Orders" to jump to the orders page.
💡
Tip: Check the Dashboard first thing every day. If you see Pending Orders or Low Stock Items with a number, those need your attention.

3. Products

This is where you manage everything that's for sale on the website. You can add new products, make changes to existing ones, or remove products that are no longer available.

How to View All Products

  1. In the left menu, click Products.
  2. You'll see a list of all your products with their name, category, price, and a photo.
  3. If you have many products, use the search bar at the top — type a product name and it will filter the list for you.
  4. Use the page buttons at the bottom to move between pages of products.

How to Add a New Product

  1. In the left menu, click Products.
  2. Click the green "Add Product" button in the top right corner.
  3. Fill in the product details — name, description, price, category, THC/CBD content.
  4. Upload a photo of the product by clicking the image area.
  5. When you're happy with everything, click the Save button at the bottom.
  6. The product will now appear on the website and in your products list.

How to Edit an Existing Product

  1. In the left menu, click Products.
  2. Find the product you want to change (use the search bar if needed).
  3. Click the Edit button next to that product.
  4. Make your changes — you can update the name, price, description, photo, or anything else.
  5. Click Save when you're done. Changes show on the website straight away.

How to Delete a Product

  1. In the left menu, click Products.
  2. Find the product you want to remove.
  3. Click the Delete button next to it.
  4. A message will pop up asking "Are you sure?" — click Confirm to permanently remove the product.
⚠️
Warning: Deleting a product cannot be undone. If you just want to temporarily hide something, rather edit it and mark it as inactive instead of deleting it.

4. Bulk Pricing

Bulk pricing lets you give customers a better deal when they buy more of something. For example, 1 item costs R100, but if they buy 5 or more, each item drops to R80. You set the rules here.

How to View Existing Bulk Pricing Rules

  1. In the left menu, click Bulk Pricing.
  2. You'll see a list of all the bulk deals currently set up — showing the product name, how many units trigger the deal, and the discounted price.
  3. Use the search bar to find a specific product's pricing rules.

How to Set Up Bulk Pricing for One Product

  1. In the left menu, click Bulk Pricing.
  2. Click the "Configure Bulk Pricing" button.
  3. A window pops up — choose "Search Product".
  4. Type the product name and click on it when it appears.
  5. Fill in the details:
    — From quantity (e.g. 2 — meaning the deal kicks in when they buy 2 or more)
    — To quantity (optional — e.g. 5, so the deal applies only from 2 to 5 units)
    — Price per unit at that quantity level (e.g. R80)
  6. Click Save to apply the deal.

How to Set Up Bulk Pricing for a Whole Category

  1. Click "Configure Bulk Pricing".
  2. Choose "Choose Subcategory" instead.
  3. Pick the category you want the deal to apply to (e.g. "Edibles").
  4. Set your quantity range and discounted price the same way as above.
  5. This will automatically apply the same deal to every product in that category.

How to Edit or Remove a Bulk Pricing Rule

  1. Find the rule in the list.
  2. Click Edit to change the quantity range or price, then save.
  3. Click Delete to completely remove the deal. A confirmation message will appear — click Confirm.
💡
Example: If a product costs R100 each normally, you could set it so buying 3–9 drops it to R85 each, and buying 10 or more drops it to R70 each. You'd create two separate rules for this.

5. Stores

This is where you manage your physical store locations. Each store can have its own stock levels and can be turned on or off independently.

How to View Your Stores

  1. In the left menu, click Stores.
  2. You'll see cards for each store showing its name, address, phone number, and how many products are in stock there.
  3. A green "Active" badge means the store is visible to customers. A grey "Inactive" badge means it's hidden.

How to Add a New Store

  1. In the left menu, click Stores.
  2. Click the "Add Store" button in the top right corner.
  3. Fill in the store details — name, address, phone number, email, and whether it offers pickup.
  4. Click Save. The new store will appear in your list.

How to Edit a Store

  1. Find the store you want to change.
  2. Click the Edit button on its card.
  3. Update any details you need to change.
  4. Click Save.

How to Turn a Store On or Off

  1. Find the store you want to toggle.
  2. Click the power button icon on the store card.
  3. If the store was Active, it will become Inactive (hidden from customers). If it was Inactive, it will become Active (visible to customers).
  4. No saving needed — it changes immediately.

How to Delete a Store

  1. Find the store you want to remove.
  2. Click the Delete button on its card.
  3. Confirm when asked. The store will be permanently removed.
💡
Tip: If a store is temporarily closed (like for a holiday), turn it Off instead of deleting it. That way all the stock and settings are still there when you turn it back on.

6. Inventory / Stock

Inventory is where you keep track of how much stock you have for each product at each store. When stock runs low, this is where you update it.

How to View Stock Levels

  1. In the left menu, click Inventory.
  2. You'll see a list showing each product, which store it's at, and how many units are in stock.
  3. Each item will have a coloured status badge:
    In Stock — plenty available   Low Stock — running low, needs restocking   No Stock — completely out
  4. Use the search bar to find a specific product, or use the Store dropdown to filter by one store.

How to Update Stock (Add, Remove, or Set a Number)

  1. Find the product and store you want to update in the list.
  2. Click the "Adjust Stock" button next to it.
  3. A window pops up with 3 options:
    — Add ➕ — adds units to what's already there (e.g. you received 50 more units)
    — Remove ➖ — removes units (e.g. stock was damaged or written off)
    — Set 📝 — sets the stock to an exact number (e.g. you counted and there are exactly 30 units)
  4. Click which action you want, type the number, and you'll see a preview of what the new stock count will be.
  5. Click Confirm to save the change.

How to Add a Product to a Store's Stock (for the First Time)

  1. In the left menu, click Inventory.
  2. Click the "Add Stock" button in the top right corner.
  3. Select the product and the store from the dropdowns.
  4. Enter the starting quantity.
  5. Click Save.
⚠️
Important: If there's a red banner at the top of the Inventory page showing "Low Stock Alerts", those products need to be restocked urgently. Don't ignore this — customers won't be able to order them.

7. Orders

When a customer places an order on the website, it shows up here. This is where you manage everything from the moment an order comes in to when it's been delivered or picked up.

How to View Orders

  1. In the left menu, click Orders.
  2. You'll see a list of all orders. At the top there are summary boxes showing totals and active orders.
  3. Each order card shows the order number, customer name, what they ordered, the total price, and the current status.

Understanding Order Statuses

  1. Processing — the order has been paid for and is being prepared.
  2. Out for Delivery — the order is on its way to the customer.
  3. Picked Up — the customer collected their order in store.
  4. Completed — the order has been successfully delivered or collected.
  5. Cancelled — the order was cancelled.
  6. Refunded — the customer was given their money back.

How to Process an Order (Move It Forward)

  1. Find the order you want to update.
  2. Click the "Manage Order" button on the order card.
  3. A window will pop up showing a button for the next step (e.g. "Mark as Out for Delivery" or "Mark as Completed").
  4. Click that button to move the order to the next stage.
  5. The order status badge will update immediately.

How to View Customer Details for an Order

  1. Find the order.
  2. Click the "Customer Information" button.
  3. You'll see the customer's name, email, phone number, and their delivery or pickup address.

How to View or Print an Invoice

  1. Find the order.
  2. Click the "View Invoice" button.
  3. The invoice will open in a new tab as a PDF. You can print it from there or save it.

How to Cancel or Refund an Order

  1. Find the order.
  2. Click "Manage Order".
  3. In the window, you'll see a "Cancel Order" or "Refund Order" button.
  4. Click it, confirm when asked, and the order status will update.

How to Filter and Search Orders

  1. Use the search bar to find an order by order number, customer name, or email.
  2. Use the date buttons (Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days) to narrow down by time.
  3. Use the Store filter to see orders from one specific store only.
  4. Use the Status filter to show only certain types of orders (e.g. just "Processing" orders that need action).
  5. Use the Fulfillment filter to separate Delivery orders from Store Pickup orders.
💡
Daily Routine: Start by filtering orders to "Processing" status — these are the ones waiting for you to take action. Work through them one by one each morning.

8. PayFast Statements

This section shows you all the money that has come in through the website's online payment system (PayFast). You can see how much was received, what fees were taken, and download a full statement if needed.

How to View Your Payment History

  1. In the left menu, click PayFast Statements.
  2. Choose what time period you want to look at:
    — Monthly — pick a specific month
    — Weekly — pick a specific week starting date
    — Daily — pick a specific day
    — Range — choose a start date and end date
  3. Click the "Fetch Statement" button.
  4. The screen will load your transactions for that period.

Understanding the Summary Cards

  1. Total Received (Gross) — the full amount customers paid (shown in green).
  2. PayFast Fees — the amount PayFast kept as their charge for processing payments (shown in orange).
  3. Total Net — what you actually received after fees (shown in blue).
  4. Account Balance — your current balance in your PayFast account (shown in purple).

How to Download a Statement

  1. Fetch a statement for the period you want (see steps above).
  2. Click the "Download CSV" button.
  3. A file will download to your computer. You can open it in Excel or send it to your accountant.

9. Settings

Settings is where you control the general information on the website — things like your contact details, social media links, delivery fees, and who has admin access.

How to Update Social Media Links

  1. In the left menu, click Settings.
  2. Find the "Social Media Links" section.
  3. Type or paste your Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, YouTube, or LinkedIn page addresses into the boxes.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and click "Save All Settings".

How to Update Contact Information

  1. In the left menu, click Settings.
  2. Find the "Contact Information" section.
  3. Update your contact email address, phone number, and WhatsApp number.
  4. Click "Save All Settings" at the bottom.

How to Change the Delivery Fee

  1. In the left menu, click Settings.
  2. Find the "Pricing Settings" section.
  3. Type the delivery fee amount in the box (in Rands). Set it to 0 for free delivery.
  4. Click "Save All Settings".

How to Turn Off Stock Checks (Allow Orders Even When Out of Stock)

  1. In the left menu, click Settings.
  2. Find the "Inventory Settings" section.
  3. Click the toggle next to "Disable Stock Checks" to turn it on.
  4. When this is on, customers can still add products to their cart even if the stock shows as 0.
  5. Click "Save All Settings".

How to Give Someone Admin Access

  1. In the left menu, click Settings.
  2. Scroll down to the "Admin Management" section.
  3. In the "Promote User to Admin" box, type the person's email address.
  4. Click "Search User".
  5. If the person has an account, their name and details will appear. Click "Make Admin".
  6. They will now have admin access the next time they log in.

How to Remove Someone's Admin Access

  1. In the left menu, click Settings.
  2. Scroll down to "Current Administrators".
  3. Find the person's name in the list.
  4. Click the "Remove Admin" button next to their name.
  5. They will lose admin access immediately.
⚠️
Reminder: Always click "Save All Settings" at the bottom of the page after making any changes in Settings — otherwise your changes won't be saved.
